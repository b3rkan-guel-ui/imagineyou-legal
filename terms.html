1. What this app is

Imagine You Uploader ("the app") is a private tool run by Berkan Gül ("the operator") for the operator's own YouTube channel "Imagine You". It creates animated "What if" explainer videos, uploads them and Shorts cut from them to that channel through the YouTube Data API, and reads the channel's own statistics and comments to improve future videos.

2. Who may use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the operator uses it, with the operator's own Google account and YouTube channel. There is no sign-up and no service for other people.

3. Content

All videos are created by the app itself: code-drawn cartoon animation, an AI text-to-speech voice and scripts written with AI assistance and checked by the operator. Videos are fictional thought experiments for entertainment and are not medical or professional advice. The operator is responsible for every published video and follows the YouTube Community Guidelines.
